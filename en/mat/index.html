The MAT

MAT was born from a real need: young people who turned a disused space into a lived place, taking risks and making an idea concrete.

Since then, countless projects and people have passed through. We are many, immersed in this space of possibility.

MAT was born from young people who turned a disused space into a lived place. Since then, projects and people: we are many, immersed in this space of possibility.

MAT means designing and living together: a shared place for creativity, growth and experimentation. Here community has room to meet, exchange and evolve with its needs.

Live music, theatre, workshops, rehearsals, assemblies: many practices, one home — for people with an idea, for makers, for anyone who wants to live their city as they have always imagined.
