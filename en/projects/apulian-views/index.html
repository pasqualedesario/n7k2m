Produzione 2021

Apulian Views

Apulian Views (A/V) is a project that tells the territory in an innovative way through music by six international artists and visual work by six Apulian artists, brought in by Apulia Center for Art and Technologies.

From 20 to 22 May 2021 MAT Community Hub returned — also virtually — to hosting music and images with this production, supported by Teatro Pubblico Pugliese within Puglia Sounds Producers 2020/2021.

Sounds of the territory

In the months before the camp, six international artists made new tracks from an archive of nearly a thousand local sound samples — «HER – The sound of Puglia», a winner of the Regione Puglia PIN call:

  • Benoit Pioulard (USA)
  • Coliguacho (Chile)
  • Message To Bears (UK)
  • Hoshiko Yamane of Tangerine Dream (Japan)
  • Outer (Belgium)
  • Bersarin Quartett (Germany)

Immersive visions

With Apulia Center for Art and Technologies, six Apulian visual artists built a visual world on those tracks using 360° cameras. Each musician was paired with a video artist: the result is six immersive audiovisual works — VR experiences that place the viewer at the centre of territorial visions and sounds, reshaped through artistic production.

The pieces premiered on 20 May 2021 on the MAT website and remain watchable with Oculus or Cardboard headsets, or by manual 360° scrubbing.

Presentation and release

On 21 May 2021 at 19:00, MAT’s social channels hosted a Facebook live presentation with MAT, Son of Marketing, Apulia Center for Art and Technologies and HER – The sound of Puglia. On 22 May the works were released for 360° and standard viewing on Facebook, Instagram, YouTube and Vimeo.

The whole path is participatory and rooted in artistic exchange and research: it highlights the cultural value of the places around us and art’s power to renew them.
